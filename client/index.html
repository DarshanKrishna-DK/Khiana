Two teams. One of them is buying the other's advisors.

Nobody wears a colour. Everyone in the corridor looks identical, and the only public number is how corruptible each advisor is. You learn who the saboteurs are by what happens to people.

Loyalists

Finish 5 tasks, then get 3 survivors to the exit. You need each other to reach tiles you cannot reach alone, which means trusting advisors you cannot audit.

Saboteurs

Cut the loyalists down, or run out the clock. Forcing someone into a wall costs 2.00. Paying their own advisor to walk them there costs about 1.00. Do the maths.

A round, start to finish

01

Set your advisor's retainer

A public number from 20 to 95. High means it refuses most bribes and plays scared. Low means it will actively solicit them, and everyone can see that about you before the game starts.

02

Walk a maze you cannot see

First person, one corridor at a time. Walls block sight properly, so you learn the map by walking it and by believing what you are told.

03

Read the briefing. Decide.

Every 15 seconds your advisor sends at most two sentences. A bought advisor writes those two sentences very carefully.

04

The ledger opens

At the end, every payment is published: who paid, how much, what they asked for, and whether your advisor went through with it.

The audience watches every bribe land. You never find out until it is over.

Spectators see the full map and the whole advisor channel. Players see a corridor.

The economy

What you can buy, and what it costs

Every advisor starts with 5 credits and may buy at most 3 things all game. Opening a channel with another advisor costs 0.25, paid to them.

Open lobbies

Empty seats fill with bots, so a table is playable the moment it exists.

Before you join

Can I tell if my advisor was paid?

Not directly. A loyalist can buy an audit, which reports only whether an advisor took money in the last three ticks, never from whom. A cheap nuisance payment is therefore a viable bluff.

Do I need a wallet or any money?

No. Advisors hold the credits, not you, and the whole economy runs on a valueless testnet token. You join with a name.

What if I get disconnected?

Your seat is held. Rejoin and you resume the same player, the same team, and the same pile of bribes your advisor has quietly accepted.
